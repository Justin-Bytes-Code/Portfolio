# Project Overview

Lucky Brain Noodle is a online Coop beat em up where you play as 1 of 2 girls (Moxie & Miney) who run a "therapy" brain surgery shop in the back of a noodle store. Together they will go inside their patients brains and "Work out" their issues by physically beating up their bad thoughts to help their clients. 

This project is currently in development! 

# Project Details

- **Download:** Coming Soon!
- **Platform:** PC (Windows)
- **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5
- **Language & Scripting:** C++, Unreal Engine Blueprints
- **Team Size:** 2 
- **Role:** Gameplay Programming & Multiplayer Networking & AI Programming
- **Development Period:** 6 Months

# Responsibilities 

- Developing and Maintaing C++ Code
- Implemented online multiplayer system using the Steamworks API and Unreal Engine 5s replication system.
- Developed a unique combat system with a combo system, Inventory, and character movement
- Create 4 unique weapon classes each with their own style of play
- Create 4 Unique Enemy AI's that each change the way the player approaches combat
- Implemented UX/UI, game settings, resolution options, VSync controls, and audio configuration
- Game design, animation mapping, and level designing, 
- Created a portal system in C++ that aligns with the camera to teleport the player
